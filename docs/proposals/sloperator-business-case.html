Why it belongs inside Muse

  • Reliable internal access. Approved network routes and service authentication remove the personal VPN/OTP dependency for connected systems. A job should not need an employee to reconnect it.
  • Company ownership. Muse controls the host, billing, credentials, backups and handover. The service continues through holidays, laptop shutdowns and personnel changes.
  • Controlled data and access. Store working files and run history on managed infrastructure, with scoped permissions, credential rotation and a defined retention policy.
  • Compounding team knowledge. Versioned definitions, tools and documented methods are available to every approved run. Improvements carry into future work.
  • Accountable automation. Dedicated identities make agent actions distinguishable from employee actions and separate automation capacity from personal AI usage.

Corporate hosting covers the execution environment. Claude and Codex still process selected inputs through external providers; IT must approve that data flow and the account arrangement. Stable internal routes remove the VPN need, not normal access controls.

Accounts and responsibility

Company-funded Claude and OpenAI access, plus dedicated bot/service identities for Slack, Jira/Confluence and approved data tools. Use vendor-supported account types and company-controlled recovery.

Proposed owners: Analytics/Product owns workflows and quality; IT owns hosting, access and recovery; Finance owns the spending limit. Human owners retain business decisions and review exceptions.
